# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate, use inclusive
language, discuss ideas rather than people, respect privacy and intellectual property, and give
extra care to patients and caregivers discussing sensitive experiences.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private
information, soliciting identifiable health data, or presenting unreviewed medical claims as
approved guidance. Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact options or
GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. Do not put sensitive personal or medical information in an issue.
