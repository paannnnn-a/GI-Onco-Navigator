# Product scope

GI-Onco Navigator is a patient-facing, evidence-grounded postoperative information navigation
platform for gastrointestinal cancer. It helps a user structure their records, understand which
postoperative phase they may be in, find relevant educational evidence, and prepare questions for
their treating team.

It does not diagnose disease, calculate an individual treatment plan, prescribe medication, select
a chemotherapy regimen, or replace professional care.

## Final product capabilities

- Structured gastrointestinal oncology patient record
- Postoperative journey state engine with missing-information detection
- PDF, webpage, and video ingestion with OCR and traceable locations
- Hybrid evidence retrieval, reranking, and citation validation
- Patient-friendly answers with evidence-class labels and uncertainty
- Red-flag and prescriptive-request safety controls
- Nutrition, activity, adverse-effect, follow-up, and hospital-navigation modules
- Content review and source-version administration
- Synthetic case benchmark and regression evaluation
- Responsive web application, API, containers, CI/CD, observability, privacy controls, and docs
